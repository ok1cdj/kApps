Ondřej Koloničný Web apps for hams

Apps for a phone that shows no colour.

Small, single-purpose Android apps for the Mudita Kompakt. Designed around e-ink from the first line of code, not ported to it afterwards.

  • No Google Services
  • No background services
  • No tracking in the apps
  • GPL-3.0

Installing

Download the APK and open it on the phone, or push it over ADB with adb install. The Kompakt will ask you to allow installs from the browser or file manager once.

Via Mudita Center

In Mudita Center, open Your Kompakt → Manage Files → App installers, add the APK, then tap it on the phone and accept the install. Needs Mudita Center 3.1.0 and MuditaOS K 1.2.0 or newer.

Staying up to date

Add the GitHub repository to Obtainium and it will pick up every new release on its own.
